An analysis of Rajasthan Royals' losses reveals two recurring structural failure modes:
1. **Death Overs Leakage (Overs 18–20):** When Trent Boult finishes his spell in overs 1–14, RR lacked an unhittable yorker specialist capable of closing games when defending 20–25 runs off 12 balls.
2. **Middle-Overs Spin Containment After Chahal/Ashwin:** In tight matches, opposing batters were able to rotate strike without taking risks, elevating the target beyond RR's reach.
3. **Late-Chase Panic at 12+ RPO:** When Samson or Parag fell, RR's lower-middle order occasionally got stranded needing boundaries off every ball without a calm specialist finisher.
